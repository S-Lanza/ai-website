About Us

Over 30 years of wealth management leadership.

Based in South Florida, Scott Lanza began his Wealth Management career at Dean Witter in 1995 and moved to SouthTrust Securities in 1997, where he earned his CFP® designation through the FSU Center for Professional Development. In 2005 he founded Lighthouse Point Financial, and by 2006 his firm’s success led Stonegate Bank to recruit him to spearhead their newly formed Wealth Management division—expanding it across 22 South Florida branches until the bank’s sale in 2017. Today, Scott is a Limited Partner of Independent Financial Partners (IFP), a Tampa-based Registered Investment Adviser and Broker-Dealer, giving clients the flexibility to hold assets at Fidelity and Charles Schwab.
